# Why We Build

Every day, people interact with strangers online.

They hire freelancers.

Trade assets.

Purchase digital products.

Exchange services.

Coordinate business agreements.

Despite technological progress, most of these interactions still rely heavily on trust.

---

## The Trust Problem

Trust is valuable.

Trust is also expensive.

To reduce risk, users often depend on:

- Marketplaces
- Escrow providers
- Payment processors
- Centralized platforms
- Reputation systems

While these systems provide value, they also introduce additional costs, complexity, and points of failure.

---

## A Better Alternative

Blockchain technology offers a unique opportunity.

Instead of trusting a platform, users can rely on transparent and verifiable rules enforced through software.

This approach does not eliminate trust entirely.

Instead, it reduces the amount of trust required between parties.

We believe this is one of the most important applications of blockchain technology.

---
