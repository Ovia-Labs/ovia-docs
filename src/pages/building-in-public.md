# Building In Public

Ovia Labs is being built in public.

Not because every detail should be public, but because transparency creates better products.

We believe that sharing progress, gathering feedback, and documenting decisions leads to stronger infrastructure and more useful products.

---

## What Building In Public Means

Building in public does not mean revealing everything.

It does not mean publishing private discussions, sensitive information, security details, or future plans before they are ready.

Instead, it means sharing the journey.

Examples include:

* Development progress
* Product updates
* Milestones
* Community feedback
* Lessons learned
* Public documentation

The goal is to make the development process more transparent and collaborative.

---

## Why We Do It

Many products are built behind closed doors.

Users only see the final result.

At Ovia Labs, we prefer a different approach.

By sharing progress publicly, we can:

* Gather feedback earlier
* Validate ideas faster
* Identify problems sooner
* Improve communication
* Build stronger products

Feedback becomes part of the development process rather than an afterthought.

---

## Community-Driven Development

Some of the best ideas come from the people who actually use the products.

ChartLabs Pulse is a good example.

The project originated from discussions and feedback within the ChartLabs community.

Rather than building based on assumptions, development began with a real problem experienced by real users.

This philosophy will continue to guide future products.

---

## Public Resources

Development activity may be shared through various public channels.

Examples include:

* Documentation updates
* Product announcements
* Development progress
* Community discussions
* Public repositories
* Educational content

These resources help users understand what is being built and why.

---

## Transparency With Responsibility

Transparency is valuable, but it must be balanced with responsibility.

Certain information should remain private.

Examples include:

* Security-sensitive details
* Private credentials
* Internal operational information
* Personal data
* Unreleased infrastructure

Building in public should never compromise user safety or platform security.

---

## Learning In Public

Not every experiment succeeds.

Not every idea becomes a product.

Part of building in public is sharing lessons learned along the way.

Progress is rarely linear.

Mistakes, iterations, and improvements are all part of the process.

By documenting that journey, we create knowledge that others can learn from as well.

---

## Long-Term Commitment

Building in public is not a marketing strategy.

It is a development philosophy.

The objective is to create a more open relationship between builders, users, and contributors.

As Ovia Labs continues to grow, transparency will remain an important part of how products are developed and improved.
