# Trust Infrastructure

The internet transformed how people communicate.

Blockchain transformed how people own and transfer value.

Yet one challenge remains largely unsolved:

Trust.

Every day, billions of dollars move between individuals, businesses, communities, and applications.

Behind nearly every transaction lies the same question:

> How can two parties coordinate without fully trusting each other?

Ovia exists to answer that question.

## The Missing Layer

The internet provides communication.

Blockchain provides ownership.

What is still missing is a universal trust layer.

Today, trust is often outsourced to intermediaries.

Examples include:

* Marketplaces
* Payment processors
* Escrow providers
* Platforms
* Reputation systems

These entities help coordinate transactions, but they also introduce costs, restrictions, and dependencies.

In many cases, they become the central point of trust.

---

## Making Trust Programmable

Ovia believes trust can be reduced through transparent infrastructure.

Instead of relying on promises, transactions can rely on predefined rules.

Instead of trusting a platform, participants can verify outcomes.

Instead of depending on intermediaries, agreements can be coordinated through software.

This concept is what we refer to as programmable trust.

---

## Trust Is Not Binary

Trust is often misunderstood.

The goal is not to eliminate trust entirely.

That is neither realistic nor desirable.

The goal is to reduce the amount of trust required.

Every reduction in uncertainty creates stronger coordination.

Every reduction in counterparty risk creates more efficient markets.

Trust infrastructure is about improving those interactions.
