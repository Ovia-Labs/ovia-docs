# Products

Ovia Labs exists to build infrastructure and products for the internet economy.

While each product serves a different purpose, they all share a common objective:

Reduce friction, improve coordination, and make digital interactions more efficient.

Rather than focusing on a single application, Ovia Labs operates as a product laboratory.

Problems are identified, solutions are designed, and infrastructure is built around real-world needs.

## Ovia Protocol

Ovia Protocol is the first infrastructure product developed by Ovia Labs.

The protocol focuses on trust-minimized settlement and escrow coordination.

Its purpose is to reduce counterparty risk during digital transactions.

Core concepts include:

* Escrow agreements
* Settlement infrastructure
* On-chain coordination
* Transparent execution
* Programmable trust

Ovia Protocol represents the foundation layer of the ecosystem.

Future products may build directly on top of this infrastructure.

---

## ChartLabs Pulse

ChartLabs Pulse is the first application product developed by Ovia Labs.

The project originated from requests and feedback provided by members of the ChartLabs community.

Users wanted a better way to:

* Track portfolios
* Monitor wallet activity
* Analyze performance
* Organize investment data

Rather than relying on existing solutions, Ovia Labs began developing a dedicated platform designed around those requirements.

ChartLabs Pulse serves as an example of how Ovia Labs transforms community needs into real products.
