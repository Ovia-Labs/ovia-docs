# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Ovia Labs?

Ovia Labs is a product and infrastructure laboratory focused on improving digital coordination.

The organization develops products, tools, and infrastructure designed to reduce friction, improve transparency, and strengthen trust across the internet economy.

---

## What is Ovia Protocol?

Ovia Protocol is the first infrastructure product developed by Ovia Labs.

The protocol provides programmable escrow and settlement infrastructure that helps reduce counterparty risk during digital transactions.

Rather than relying entirely on trust between participants, transactions are coordinated through predefined rules enforced by smart contracts.

---

## Is Ovia a blockchain?

No.

Ovia is not a blockchain.

Ovia Protocol is infrastructure built on top of existing blockchain networks.

The protocol focuses on settlement and coordination rather than consensus or block production.

---

## Is Ovia a marketplace?

No.

Ovia Protocol is not a marketplace.

The protocol is designed as infrastructure that marketplaces, applications, businesses, and communities can build on top of.

---

## Who controls funds held in escrow?

Funds are held by smart contracts according to the rules defined within each agreement.

Ovia Labs does not custody user funds.

The protocol is designed to minimize reliance on centralized control.

---

## What types of transactions can use Ovia Protocol?

Potential use cases include:

* Freelance payments
* Service agreements
* Digital product sales
* Milestone payments
* OTC transactions
* Community marketplaces
* Business agreements

Any transaction involving value exchange and counterparty risk may benefit from escrow infrastructure.

---

## What is ChartLabs Pulse?

ChartLabs Pulse is the first application product developed by Ovia Labs.

The platform focuses on portfolio tracking, wallet monitoring, and portfolio analytics for crypto users.

The product originated from requests and feedback provided by members of the ChartLabs community.

---

## Is ChartLabs Pulse part of Ovia Protocol?

No.

ChartLabs Pulse and Ovia Protocol serve different purposes.

Ovia Protocol is infrastructure.

ChartLabs Pulse is an application.

Both are developed by Ovia Labs and contribute to the broader ecosystem.

---

## Why was ChartLabs Pulse built first?

The ChartLabs community identified a real problem that needed solving.

Members wanted a better way to track portfolios, monitor wallet activity, and organize investment data.

Ovia Labs chose to solve that problem through the development of ChartLabs Pulse.

The project serves as an example of how Ovia Labs transforms community feedback into real products.

---

## Is Ovia Labs open source?

Certain components may be open source.

Others may remain private.

Ovia Labs believes in transparency, but transparency does not require every component to be publicly accessible.

The approach may evolve as products and infrastructure mature.

---

## What does "Building in Public" mean?

Building in public means sharing progress, updates, lessons, and development milestones with the community.

It does not mean publishing sensitive information, private discussions, security details, or proprietary infrastructure.

The goal is transparency without compromising security or long-term development.
