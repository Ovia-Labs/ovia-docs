# Use Cases

Ovia Protocol is designed as flexible escrow infrastructure.

Rather than focusing on a single industry or application, the protocol provides a trust-minimized settlement layer that can be used across a wide range of digital transactions.

Any situation involving two or more parties exchanging value can potentially benefit from programmable escrow.

---

## Freelancer Payments

One of the most common use cases for Ovia Protocol is freelance work.

Today, freelancers often face a difficult choice:

* Begin work before receiving payment
* Demand payment before delivering work

Both approaches introduce risk.

With Ovia Protocol:

1. The client creates an agreement
2. Funds are deposited into escrow
3. The freelancer verifies payment
4. Work is completed
5. Funds are released

This creates a more balanced transaction environment for both parties.

---

## Digital Product Sales

Digital products are often difficult to refund once delivered.

Examples include:

* Design files
* Software licenses
* Templates
* Digital art
* Educational content

Ovia Protocol allows buyers and sellers to coordinate delivery and payment through predefined settlement logic.

This reduces uncertainty during the transaction process.

## Milestone Payments

Large projects are rarely completed in a single step.

Many projects are divided into milestones.

Examples include:

* Software development
* Website creation
* Consulting engagements
* Content production
* Marketing campaigns

Using Ovia Protocol, each milestone can have its own escrow agreement.

```text
Project
│
├── Milestone 1
│   └── Escrow #1
│
├── Milestone 2
│   └── Escrow #2
│
└── Milestone 3
    └── Escrow #3
```

This allows payments to be released progressively as work is completed.

Instead of trusting the other participant directly, both parties rely on transparent escrow logic.
