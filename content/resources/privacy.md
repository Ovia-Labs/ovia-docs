This notice explains how Ovia Labs handles the information you send through the [contributor application form](https://ovialabs.xyz/join).

## Who is responsible

Ovia Labs. You can reach us at contact@ovialabs.xyz.

## What we collect

Only what you enter in the form: your name, email address, preferred role, motivation and, if you choose, your experience, one link and your preferred involvement level. We never ask for seed phrases, private keys, wallet connections or identity documents.

## Why we use it

To review your application, contact you about it and, if we both want to, discuss a possible collaboration.

## Legal basis

Taking the steps you ask for before a possible collaboration, and our legitimate interest in reviewing the applications we receive.

## Who receives it

Your application is delivered to the Ovia Labs inbox through our email delivery provider, Resend, and the site runs on Cloudflare. These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area under their own data protection terms. We never publish your application or share it with anyone else.

## How long we keep it

We keep applications only as long as needed to review them and stay in touch about them, and no longer than 12 months after our last contact, unless you ask us to keep them longer.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, object to its use, or ask for a copy at any time. Email contact@ovialabs.xyz. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.
