## v0.2: Ovia Protect public beta

**18 November 2026 · Draft**

- Ovia Protect went live as a public beta at [protect.ovialabs.xyz](https://protect.ovialabs.xyz). Its status changed from PRD Phase to Public Beta throughout the documentation.
- Rewrote the Ovia Protect pages (overview, user experience, scope, technical design, security and privacy) to describe the released beta, including the RPC providers it uses.
- Roadmap: Phase 01 and Phase 02 marked completed, Phase 03 in progress. Added revoking with the user’s own wallet as the next planned step.
- Whitepaper v0.2: updated chapters 1, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10 and 11. Open questions settled by the beta are marked as answered.
- **Changed requirement:** whitepaper v0.1 required accuracy testing, coverage testing and an independent review before any public release. Ovia Protect was released as a public beta first; these requirements now apply before it leaves beta. None of them is reported as completed.
- The website and the documentation are now live.

## v0.1: Initial documentation release

**17 November 2026 · Draft**

- Published the documentation site structure: Getting Started, Whitepaper, Ovia Protect, Ovia Network, OVIA Token, Roadmap, Developers and Resources.
- Published whitepaper v0.1, “Ovia Labs — Building a More Verifiable Web3”, in 12 chapters.
- Documented the PRD-010 data-source feasibility findings for Ovia Protect.
- Introduced the status legend used across the documentation.
- Published the Security Disclosures process and the applicant privacy notice (draft).

## How changes are recorded

- **Status changes** (for example, a milestone moving from Planned to Completed) are recorded with the evidence for them.
- **Corrections** are listed explicitly, including what was wrong.
- **Removed claims** are noted, not silently deleted.
