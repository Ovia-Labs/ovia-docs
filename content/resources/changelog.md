## v0.1: Initial documentation release

**17 November 2026 · Draft**

- Published the documentation site structure: Getting Started, Whitepaper, Ovia Protect, Ovia Network, OVIA Token, Roadmap, Developers and Resources.
- Published whitepaper v0.1, “Ovia Labs — Building a More Verifiable Web3”, in 12 chapters.

- Published the Security Disclosures process and the applicant privacy notice (draft).

## How changes are recorded

- **Status changes** (for example, a milestone moving from Planned to Completed) are recorded with the evidence for them.
- **Corrections** are listed explicitly, including what was wrong.
- **Removed claims** are noted, not silently deleted.
