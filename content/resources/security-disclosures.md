Ovia Labs welcomes reports of security issues in its public websites and documentation. There are currently no deployed products or smart contracts.

## How to report

Email **contact@ovialabs.xyz** with the subject line starting `Security:`. Please include:

- what you found and where (URL or component);
- steps to reproduce;
- the impact you believe it has;
- whether you would like to be credited, and how.

Please do not include real users’ personal data, private keys or seed phrases.

## Scope

| In scope | Out of scope |
|---|---|
| ovialabs.xyz | Third-party services we use (report to them directly) |
| docs.ovialabs.xyz | Social engineering of people |
| Forms and server functions on these sites | Denial-of-service or load testing |
| | Reports from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact |

## Guidelines

- Test only against your own data and accounts.
- Do not access, modify or delete other people’s data.
- Give us reasonable time to fix an issue before disclosing it publicly.

## What to expect

We aim to acknowledge reports and keep you updated on progress. Ovia Labs is a small team, so we do not publish fixed response times.

## Rewards

There is no paid bug bounty programme at this time. With your permission, we are happy to credit you for a valid report.

## Impersonation

Ovia Labs will never ask for your seed phrase, private key or a signature by email, direct message or form, and has not issued any token. Please report impersonation to the same address.
