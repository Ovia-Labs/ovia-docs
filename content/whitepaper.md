## About this document

This whitepaper describes what Ovia Labs is building, why, and in what order. It is a technical and product document, not a token sale document. It does not offer anything for sale and makes no promise of financial return.

Version 0.1 is an initial draft, published while Ovia Protect is in the requirements phase. Large parts of it describe proposals and open research questions. We publish it early so the reasoning can be reviewed before it hardens into decisions.

## How statements are classified

Every major statement in this paper falls into one of five categories. In the text, the category is either stated directly or shown with a short status label.

| Category | Meaning | Typical label |
|---|---|---|
| Established fact | A verified, current condition | — |
| Confirmed decision | A documented Ovia Labs decision | — |
| Technical proposal | A design that has not been implemented or validated | {{status:proposed}} |
| Future vision | A longer-term objective | {{status:future}} |
| Open question | An unresolved decision or research problem | {{status:not-specified}} |

When this paper says “Ovia Protect will…”, read it as “the current plan is that Ovia Protect will…”. Only the items listed as confirmed in [Current Development Status](/status) are decided.
